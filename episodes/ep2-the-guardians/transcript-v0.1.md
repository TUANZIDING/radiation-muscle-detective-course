## 1  The cell on the edge · 一  边缘上的细胞

## 2  Proof of necessity · 二  必要性的证明


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Relaix & Zammit 2012 Development; Lepper 2011 Development</sub>

- For fifty years, the satellite cell was the leading suspect in muscle repair. But a suspect is not yet a proven culprit.  
  五十年来，卫星细胞一直是肌肉修复的头号嫌疑人。但嫌疑人还不等于被证实的那个人。
- Over those years, cells from other origins were also reported to join muscle fibers after they were transplanted.  
  这些年里，也有报道说，来自其他来源的细胞移植后同样能加入肌纤维。
- So a fair question lingered. Were satellite cells truly required, or were they one helper among many?  
  于是一个合理的问题一直悬着：卫星细胞真的不可缺少吗？还是只是众多帮手之一？

<sub>参考：Lepper 2011; Sambasivan 2011; Murphy 2011 Development</sub>

- There is only one honest way to settle such a question. Remove the suspect, and see whether the work still gets done.  
  要回答这种问题，只有一个老实办法：把嫌疑人拿走，看看活儿还能不能干成。
- In September 2011, three independent teams did exactly that, and their papers appeared side by side in the same issue of the journal Development.

  在犹他，Malea Murphy、Gabrielle Kardon 和同事用又一种基因开关清除了卫星细胞。

## 3  Quiescence is not sleep · 三  静止不是沉睡


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Cheung & Rando 2013 Nat Rev Mol Cell Biol</sub>

- Now, what do the guardians do when nothing is wrong?  
  那么，天下太平的时候，守护者在做什么？
- In an adult muscle at rest, most satellite cells are not dividing. Biologists call this state quiescence.  
  在静息的成年肌肉里，大多数卫星细胞并不分裂。生物学家把这种状态叫做静止。
- For a long time it was pictured as dormancy, a cell asleep in its bunk, doing as little as possible.  
  很长一段时间里，人们把它想象成休眠：细胞躺在铺位上睡觉，尽量什么都不做。
- In 2013, Tom Cheung and Thomas Rando reviewed the evidence for a different picture.  
  2013 年，Tom Cheung 和 Thomas Rando 综述了支持另一幅图景的证据。
- Quiescence, they argued, is actively maintained. Signaling pathways and gene controls hold the cell in a poised state.  
  他们认为，静止是被主动维持的：信号通路和基因调控把细胞保持在一种蓄势待发的状态。
- Think of a fire crew at the station. The engine is not racing, but the boots are standing by the door.  
  想想消防站里的消防队：发动机没在轰鸣，但靴子就摆在门口。
- That readiness allows rapid activation when the alarm comes, and the state can be reversed.  
  这种准备就绪，让警报一响就能迅速激活；而且这种状态是可逆的。
- When the muscle is injured, the guardian wakes. It leaves quiescence and begins to divide.  
  肌肉一受伤，守护者就醒来，离开静止状态，开始分裂。
- Its daughters multiply into a crowd of myoblasts, which fuse to repair damaged fibers or to build new ones.  
  它的子代繁殖成一大群成肌细胞，它们融合起来，修补受损的纤维，或者造出新的纤维。
- And here is what makes it a stem cell. Some daughters do not fuse. They return to the niche and go quiet again.  
  而让它称得上干细胞的，是这一点：有些子代并不融合，而是回到微环境里，重新安静下来。
- This is called self-renewal. Every repair must leave behind guardians for the next one.  
  这叫自我更新。每一次修复，都必须为下一次留下守护者。

<sub>参考：Vahidi Ferdousi 2014 Stem Cell Res; Caiozzo 2010 Radiat Res</sub>

- Now hold that cycle up beside our puzzle.  
  现在，把这个循环和我们的谜题放在一起看。
- Vahidi Ferdousi and her colleagues showed that quiescent satellite cells repair radiation breaks well, using the welder's tool, DNA-PKcs.  
  Vahidi Ferdousi 和同事证明，静止的卫星细胞能很好地修复辐射造成的断裂，用的是焊工的工具 DNA-PKcs。
- But in 2010, Vincent Caiozzo, Charles Limoli and their colleagues irradiated satellite cells growing in culture, where they divide.  
  可是在 2010 年，Vincent Caiozzo、Charles Limoli 和同事照射了在培养皿里生长、正在分裂的卫星细胞。
- Doses of five grays or more reduced their numbers by at least seventy percent.  
  5 Gy 及以上的剂量，让细胞数量减少了至少七成。
- Part of the loss came from apoptosis, and part from cells stalling in the cell cycle.  
  一部分损失来自凋亡，一部分来自细胞周期停滞。
- Radiation also kept reactive oxygen and nitrogen species high, while nitric oxide, which these cells need to proliferate, fell.  
  辐射还让活性氧和活性氮持续升高，而这些细胞增殖所需要的一氧化氮却下降了。
- So the same family of cells looks sturdy at rest and fragile when it is busy dividing.  
  所以，同一家族的细胞，静息时看起来很结实，忙着分裂时却很脆弱。
- Keep that contrast in mind. It is the hinge on which our answer will turn.  
  请记住这个对比。我们的答案，就要绕着这根门轴转。

- A note from the bench. How do you know whether a cell has been dividing?  
  一段实验台笔记：怎么知道一个细胞有没有分裂过？

## 4  The hidden reserve · 四  隐藏的储备


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Heslop 2000 J Cell Sci</sub>

- Now we bring radiation into the room.  
  现在，让射线登场。
- In 2000, Heslop, Jennifer Morgan and Terence Partridge irradiated mouse muscle with eighteen grays.  
  2000 年，Heslop、Jennifer Morgan 和 Terence Partridge 用 18 Gy 照射了小鼠肌肉。
- They studied normal mice, and also mdx mice, a model of Duchenne muscular dystrophy.  
  他们研究了正常小鼠，也研究了 mdx 小鼠，一种杜氏肌营养不良的模型。
- After irradiation alone, neither normal nor dystrophic muscle regenerated to any significant extent.  
  单纯照射之后，无论正常肌肉还是营养不良的肌肉，都没有明显再生。
- Single fibers lifted out of that muscle released very few precursor cells, and those barely divided.  
  从这些肌肉里取出的单根纤维，只释放出极少的前体细胞，而且它们几乎不分裂。
- Then the team injected notexin, a snake venom toxin that severely damages muscle.  
  接着，团队注射了 notexin，一种严重破坏肌肉的蛇毒毒素。
- To their surprise, the irradiated muscles regenerated.  
  出乎他们意料，受过照射的肌肉再生了。
- Some single fibers now produced rapidly dividing precursor cells, more often from normal muscle than from dystrophic muscle.  
  有些单根纤维这时能产生快速分裂的前体细胞，而且正常肌肉比营养不良肌肉更常见。
- Even after twenty-five grays, notexin evoked some new muscle, though no dividing precursor cells remained on the fibers.  
  即使照射 25 Gy，notexin 仍能唤起一些新肌肉，只是纤维上已经没有能分裂的前体细胞了。
- Their conclusion: muscle holds distinct populations of myogenic cells. Most are radiation sensitive, but some survive and wait for an extreme emergency.  
  他们的结论是：肌肉里有几类功能不同的成肌细胞。大多数对辐射敏感，但有一些能活下来，等待极端的紧急情况。
- And in dystrophic muscle, that hidden reserve was markedly diminished, as if a long war had already spent it.  
  而在营养不良的肌肉里，这支隐藏的储备明显减少了，好像一场漫长的战争早已把它耗光。

<sub>参考：Wernig 2000 J Physiol; Boldrin 2012 Stem Cells</sub>

- Scientists soon turned this weakness into a tool.  
  科学家很快把这个弱点变成了工具。
- In 2000, Anton Wernig and his colleagues gave sixteen grays of X-rays to the mouse soleus before injecting donor myoblasts.  
  2000 年，Anton Wernig 和同事先用 16 Gy 的 X 射线照射小鼠比目鱼肌，再注射供体成肌细胞。
- Irradiation alone left those muscles about eighteen percent lighter and weaker. The donor cells abolished those deficits.  
  单纯照射让这些肌肉的重量和力量都下降了约 18%，而供体细胞消除了这些缺陷。
- The authors concluded that irradiation is an effective way to prepare a muscle for transplanted cells.  
  作者的结论是：照射是为细胞移植预先处理肌肉的有效办法。
- In 2012, Luisa Boldrin, Jennifer Morgan and their colleagues sharpened the rule.  
  2012 年，Luisa Boldrin、Jennifer Morgan 和同事把这条规律说得更精确。
- Donor satellite cells engrafted best when the host's own satellite cells were incapacitated, but the niche itself was preserved.  
  当宿主自己的卫星细胞失去能力、而微环境本身被保留时，供体卫星细胞植入得最好。
- If the niche was destroyed, or still filled with competent host cells, engraftment was poor. The empty house must still be standing.  
  如果微环境被毁，或者仍被有能力的宿主细胞占着，植入就很差。空房子，得是还立着的房子。

<sub>参考：Scaramozza 2019 Cell Stem Cell</sub>

- But who exactly were Heslop's survivors? For nearly twenty years, they had no name.  
  可是，Heslop 看到的幸存者究竟是谁？将近二十年里，它们都没有名字。
- In 2019, Annarita Scaramozza, Andrew Brack and their colleagues used lineage tracing to look.  
  2019 年，Annarita Scaramozza、Andrew Brack 和同事用谱系示踪去找它们。
- A genetic switch called Mx1-Cre permanently marked a small subset of Pax7-positive satellite cells.  
  一个叫 Mx1-Cre 的基因开关，永久标记了 Pax7 阳性卫星细胞中的一小群。
- These marked cells were enriched for a related gene, Pax3, and carried lower levels of reactive oxygen species.  
  这些被标记的细胞富含一个近亲基因 Pax3，体内活性氧水平也更低。
- In ordinary repair they contributed little, simply because there were so few of them.  
  在普通的修复里，它们贡献很小，只因为数量太少。
- But under the pressure of radiation, they expanded dramatically, clone by clone, rebuilding muscle and repopulating the niche.  
  可在辐射的压力下，它们一个克隆接一个克隆地大规模扩增，重建肌肉，重新填满微环境。
- When the team removed Pax3 from these reserve cells, their reactive oxygen rose, and their survival and stress tolerance fell.  
  当团队从这些储备细胞里去掉 Pax3，它们的活性氧升高，存活能力和抗压能力都下降了。
- So the guardians are not one uniform regiment. Some are a reserve, built to outlast the storm.  
  所以，守护者并不是清一色的一个团。其中有一部分是预备队，生来就是为了熬过风暴。

**🔵 实验台笔记 BENCH NOTE** · <sub>参考：Heslop 2000; Scaramozza 2019</sub>

- A note from the bench. Heslop's method is still a favorite: the single-fiber culture.  
  一段实验台笔记：Heslop 用的方法至今仍很受欢迎，那就是单纤维培养。
- Digest the muscle gently with collagenase, and single fibers come free with their satellite cells still under the sleeve.  
  用胶原酶温和地消化肌肉，单根纤维就会游离出来，卫星细胞仍留在袖套下面。
- Float the fibers in medium, and you can watch the guardians wake, divide, and crawl away, fiber by fiber.  
  把纤维漂在培养液里，你就能一根一根地看着守护者醒来、分裂、爬走。
- To collect satellite cells in bulk, labs sort them by flow cytometry, for example keeping alpha-7 integrin positive cells and excluding blood and vessel cells.  
  要大批收集卫星细胞，实验室会用流式细胞术分选，比如保留 α7 整合素阳性的细胞，排除血细胞和血管细胞。
- But remember, digestion and sorting themselves begin to wake the cells. A freshly isolated guardian is no longer quite at rest.  
  但要记住，消化和分选本身就会开始唤醒细胞。刚分离出来的守护者，已经不完全处于静息了。

## 5  The children · 五  孩子们


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Bachman 2020 iScience</sub>

- Now we come to the most painful part of tonight's story.  
  现在，我们来到今晚故事里最让人心痛的部分。
- In a child, muscle is not merely being maintained. It is being built.  
  在孩子身上，肌肉不只是在维持，而是在建造。
- Before puberty, satellite cells actively contribute to fiber growth, and some of them are busy at that age.  
  青春期之前，卫星细胞积极参与纤维的生长，其中一些在这个年纪正忙着干活。
- In 2020, Bachman, Joe Chakkalakal and their colleagues asked what radiation does to them at that age.  
  2020 年，Bachman、Joe Chakkalakal 和同事追问：在这个年纪，射线会对它们做什么？
- Using a small animal radiation research platform, they gave prepubertal mice a local, fractionated course of radiation.  
  他们用一台小动物放射研究平台，给青春期前的小鼠做了局部、分次的照射。
- Right after treatment, satellite cell numbers fell. The survivors worked less well, though some myogenic potential remained.  
  照射一结束，卫星细胞数量就下降了。幸存者功能变差，但仍保留了一些成肌潜能。
- Fourteen months later, fibers were smaller and held fewer nuclei, in both fast and slow muscles.  
  十四个月后，无论快肌还是慢肌，纤维都更细，细胞核也更少。
- And when the team depleted satellite cells genetically before puberty, with no radiation at all, the same lifelong deficits appeared.  
  而当团队在青春期前用基因手段清除卫星细胞、完全不照射时，同样的终身缺陷也出现了。
- That second experiment matters most. It points directly at the guardians.  
  第二个实验最关键，它直接指向了守护者。

<sub>参考：Kallenbach 2022 J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle; Paris 2020 Sci Rep</sub>

- In 2022, Kallenbach and the same group gave three doses of eight point two grays to the hindlimbs of four-week-old mice.  
  2022 年，Kallenbach 和同一团队给四周龄小鼠的后肢照射了三次，每次 8.2 Gy。
- Some of those mice carried a childhood muscle cancer, rhabdomyosarcoma, which the radiation eliminated.  
  其中一些小鼠身上长着一种儿童肌肉肿瘤，横纹肌肉瘤，照射把它消灭了。
- With radiation alone, by eight weeks of age, muscle mass was down seventeen percent, and muscle function twenty-five.  
  单纯照射，到八周龄时，肌肉质量下降 17%，肌肉功能下降 25%。
- Satellite cells were down forty-five percent, and fiber cross-sectional area thirty percent.  
  卫星细胞减少 45%，纤维横截面积减少 30%。
- At sixteen months, middle age for a mouse, the fibers were still shrunken, nuclei were still missing, and the fibrosis was lifelong.  
  到十六个月，也就是小鼠的中年，纤维依然萎缩，细胞核依然缺失，纤维化终身存在。
- A related study found that blocked fiber growth is a consistent feature of pediatric cancer treatment in these models.  
  一项相关研究发现，在这些模型里，纤维生长受阻是儿童肿瘤治疗的一个一贯特征。

<sub>参考：Collao 2023 J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle; Zeng 2022 Radiat Res</sub>

- But a careful detective checks whether every witness tells the same story.  
  可细心的侦探要核对：每个证人讲的是不是同一个故事。
- Collao, De Lisio and their colleagues gave a single dose of sixteen grays to one hindlimb of four-week-old mice, shielding the other.  
  Collao、De Lisio 和同事给四周龄小鼠的一侧后肢单次照射 16 Gy，另一侧遮挡。
- They found fewer satellite cells at three days, and again at fifty-six days.  
  他们发现，第 3 天卫星细胞变少，第 56 天又一次变少。
- Yet in rats given ninety grays in one shot, a team led by Zeng counted more satellite cells, not fewer, across twenty-four weeks.  
  可是在一次照射 90 Gy 的大鼠里，Zeng 领衔的团队在 24 周里数到的卫星细胞反而更多，而不是更少。
- A different species, a far higher single dose, and a different clock.  
  物种不同，单次剂量高得多，时间线也不同。
- And more satellite cells is not the same as successful repair. Fibrosis rose in those rats all the same.  
  而且，卫星细胞多，不等于修复成功。那些大鼠的纤维化照样在加重。
- So we cannot fold these results into one tidy story. Species, age, dose, and time all change what the guardians do.  
  所以，我们不能把这些结果揉成一个整齐的故事。物种、年龄、剂量和时间，都会改变守护者的表现。

<sub>参考：Vahidi Ferdousi 2014; Bachman 2020; Kallenbach 2022; Collao 2023</sub>

- So let us return to our puzzle and build an answer, one piece at a time, each with its honest label.  
  那就回到我们的谜题，一块一块地拼出答案，每一块都贴上老实的标签。
- First, the facts. Quiescent satellite cells repair DNA breaks well. And in irradiated young mice, their numbers still fall.  
  先摆事实：静止的卫星细胞修复 DNA 断裂的能力很强；可在受照的幼鼠里，它们的数量仍然下降。
- These two facts do not contradict each other, because mending a break is not the same as surviving everything that comes after it.  
  这两个事实并不矛盾，因为修好一处断口，不等于能熬过随后的一切。

**🔴 假说 HYPOTHESIS** · <sub>参考：Bachman 2020; Caiozzo 2010</sub>

- Here is the first piece of the answer, and it is a hypothesis. In growing muscle, many guardians are not resting. They are at work.  
  答案的第一块是一个假说：在生长中的肌肉里，很多守护者并不在静息，而是在干活。
- Bachman's team proposed exactly this: because some satellite cells are active before puberty, they may be especially susceptible.  
  Bachman 团队正是这样提出的：因为青春期前有些卫星细胞处于活跃状态，它们可能格外容易受伤。
- And dividing satellite cells in a dish die or stall after a few grays, just as we saw.  
  而正如我们看到的，培养皿里正在分裂的卫星细胞，挨上几 Gy 就会死亡或停滞。

**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Heslop 2000; Bachman 2020; Collao 2025 Am J Physiol Cell Physiol</sub>

- The second piece is firmer. Survivors often fail to perform. Heslop's fibers released few cells, and Bachman's survivors worked less well.  
  第二块更扎实：幸存者常常不能履职。Heslop 的纤维只放出很少的细胞，Bachman 的幸存者功能也变差了。
- A guardian that is alive but cannot answer the alarm is, to the muscle, nearly as good as gone.  
  一个活着却应不了警报的守护者，对肌肉来说，几乎等于不在了。

**🟠 关联 ASSOCIATION**

- The third piece is an association. Collao's single-cell study found a senescence signature, the p21 gene, in every type of resident muscle cell after radiation.  
  第三块是一个关联：Collao 的单细胞研究发现，照射后，肌肉里每一类常驻细胞都带着衰老印记，也就是 p21 基因。
- It also found a long-term relative loss of the most deeply quiescent satellite cells, the kind of reserve that Heslop and Scaramozza described.  
  它还发现，处于最深静止状态的卫星细胞长期相对减少，那正是 Heslop 和 Scaramozza 描述的那种储备。
- A gene signature is a witness, not a confession. It tells us senescence travels with the damage, not yet that it causes it.  
  基因印记是证人，不是供词。它告诉我们衰老与损伤同行，却还没证明是衰老造成了损伤。

**🔴 假说 HYPOTHESIS** · <sub>参考：Collao 2023; Murphy 2011</sub>

- The last piece points beyond the guardians. In Collao's 2023 study, signals from irradiated neighbor cells impaired satellite cell differentiation and fusion in a dish.  
  最后一块指向了守护者之外：在 Collao 2023 年的研究里，受照邻居细胞释放的信号，在培养皿里损害了卫星细胞的分化和融合。
- Whether a hostile neighborhood is what silences the guardians inside a living muscle is still a hypothesis.  
  在活体肌肉里，究竟是不是一个充满敌意的街区让守护者沉默，目前仍是假说。

<sub>参考：Bachman 2020; Kallenbach 2022</sub>

- So here is where tonight leaves us.  
  今晚，我们就停在这里。
- Fewer guardians helps explain why the muscle grows thin. A child's fibers stay small because builders were lost while the house was still going up.  
  守护者变少，能帮助解释肌肉为什么变细：孩子的纤维一直偏细，是因为房子还在盖，工匠就没了。
- But it does not explain why the muscle grows hard.  
  但它解释不了，肌肉为什么会变硬。

<sub>参考：Sambasivan 2011; Murphy 2011</sub>

- Remember the mice of 2011. When the guardians vanished, connective tissue, fat, and inflammatory cells moved in.  
  还记得 2011 年的那些小鼠吗？守护者一消失，结缔组织、脂肪和炎症细胞就搬了进来。
- Somebody laid that scar. And the guardians were never alone in the niche.  
  那道瘢痕，是有人铺下的。而守护者在微环境里，从来不是孤身一人。
- They live beside fibroblast-like builders, immune clean-up crews, the small vessels that feed them, and the power plants inside every fiber.  
  它们身边有像成纤维细胞那样的建筑工、负责清扫的免疫细胞、给它们供给养分的小血管，还有每根纤维里的发电厂。
- In the next episode, the neighbors turn.  
  下一集，邻居变脸。
- We will meet the fibro-adipogenic progenitors, the macrophages, a signal called TGF-beta, the vessels, and the mitochondria.  
  我们会见到成纤维-成脂祖细胞、巨噬细胞、一种叫 TGF-β 的信号、血管，还有线粒体。
- Until then, my friends, remember that a missing witness is evidence too.  
  在那之前，朋友们，请记住：缺席的证人，本身也是证据。

  本集的参考文献，都在屏幕上。
