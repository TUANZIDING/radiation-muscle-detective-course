## 1  The two faces of the FAP · 一  FAP 的两副面孔


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Joe 2010 Nat Cell Biol</sub>

- Between the muscle fibers, outside the stem cells' little chambers, lives a quiet population of cells.  
  在肌纤维之间，在干细胞那小小的居室之外，住着一群安静的细胞。
- For years, most people lumped them together as fibroblasts, the plain workmen of connective tissue.  
  多年来，大多数人把它们笼统地叫作成纤维细胞，也就是结缔组织里的普通工匠。
- In 2010, two laboratories, in the same issue of Nature Cell Biology, looked at them more closely.  
  2010 年，两个实验室在同一期《自然·细胞生物学》上，把它们看得更仔细了。
- In Vancouver, Aaron Joe, Fabio Rossi and their colleagues gave these cells a name: fibro-adipogenic progenitors, or FAPs.  
  在温哥华，Aaron Joe、Fabio Rossi 和同事给这些细胞起了个名字：纤维-脂肪生成祖细胞，简称 FAP。
- The name tells you what they can become. Fibrous tissue, and fat.  
  这个名字告诉你它们能变成什么：纤维组织，和脂肪。
- They live in muscle, but they come from a different developmental family than the satellite cells.  
  它们住在肌肉里，但和卫星细胞出自不同的发育家族。
- In healthy, intact muscle, FAPs sit quietly. When the muscle is damaged, they multiply quickly.  
  在完好的健康肌肉里，FAP 安静待着；肌肉一受伤，它们就迅速增殖。
- They never become muscle fibers themselves. But in a dish, they made muscle progenitors mature faster.  
  它们自己从不变成肌纤维；但在培养皿里，它们能让肌肉祖细胞更快地成熟。
- The authors concluded that FAPs expand after damage to provide a temporary source of signals that help the builders finish.  
  作者的结论是：FAP 在损伤后扩增，提供一股暂时的信号，帮建造者把活干完。
- Remember that word, temporary. A helper who stays too long becomes a problem.  
  请记住“暂时”这个词。帮手待得太久，就成了麻烦。

  Joe 把纯化的 FAP 移植进容易发生脂肪浸润的肌肉，它们长成了白色脂肪；移进健康肌肉，则不会。

  所以 FAP 有两副面孔：在健康纤维和忙碌的干细胞旁边，它是建造者；独自留在破败的房子里，它就成了制造脂肪和瘢痕的人。

## 2  The scavengers and their timing · 二  清道夫与时机


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Arnold 2007 J Exp Med</sub>

- Every crime scene needs a cleanup crew. In muscle, that crew is made of monocytes and macrophages.  
  每个犯罪现场都需要清理队。在肌肉里，这支队伍由单核细胞和巨噬细胞组成。
- Macrophage means big eater. They swallow dead tissue, and they talk to everyone around them.  
  巨噬细胞的意思就是“大胃王”。它们吞掉死去的组织，还和周围每个细胞交谈。
- In 2007, Ludovic Arnold, Bénédicte Chazaud and their colleagues followed this crew in injured mouse muscle.  
  2007 年，Ludovic Arnold、Bénédicte Chazaud 和同事在受伤的小鼠肌肉里追踪了这支队伍。
- First, inflammatory monocytes, carrying a marker called Ly6C, arrived from the blood.  
  首先，带有 Ly6C 标记的炎症性单核细胞从血液里赶来。
- They ate the debris. And eating the debris itself flipped a switch.  
  它们吞食碎片，而吞食碎片这个动作本身，就扳动了一个开关。
- Inside the muscle, they turned into anti-inflammatory macrophages that released a signal called TGF-beta one.  
  在肌肉内部，它们转变成抗炎巨噬细胞，并释放一种叫 TGF-β1 的信号。
- The first wave urged muscle cells to multiply. The second urged them to mature and fuse.  
  第一波促使肌肉细胞增殖，第二波促使它们成熟、融合。
- When the authors removed circulating monocytes at the moment of injury, the muscle did not regenerate at all.  
  作者在受伤那一刻清除血液中的单核细胞后，肌肉完全没有再生。
- So the cleanup crew is not optional. Repair depends on it, and on its changing its coat at the right time.  
  所以清理队不是可有可无的。修复依赖它，也依赖它在对的时间换上另一身制服。

<sub>参考：Lemos 2015 Nat Med</sub>

- Now put the cleanup crew and the FAPs in the same room.  
  现在，把清理队和 FAP 放进同一个房间。
- In 2015, Dario Lemos, working in Rossi's laboratory, showed how the two interact.  
  2015 年，Rossi 实验室的 Dario Lemos 揭示了二者如何互动。
- Early after an acute injury, inflammatory macrophages make TNF, and TNF directly drives surplus FAPs to die by apoptosis.  
  急性损伤后早期，炎症巨噬细胞分泌 TNF，TNF 直接促使多余的 FAP 凋亡。
- That is the helper being sent home once the job is done.  
  这就是活干完了，把帮手送回家。
- But in chronic damage, as in the dystrophic mdx mouse, macrophages make high levels of TGF-beta one instead.  
  可在慢性损伤里，比如营养不良的 mdx 小鼠，巨噬细胞改为大量分泌 TGF-β1。
- TGF-beta one keeps the FAPs alive, and turns them into cells that pour out matrix. That is scar.  
  TGF-β1 让 FAP 活下来，并把它们变成大量分泌基质的细胞。那就是瘢痕。
- A kinase inhibitor called nilotinib blocked that effect and reduced fibrosis in mdx mice.  
  一种叫尼洛替尼的激酶抑制剂阻断了这一作用，减轻了 mdx 小鼠的纤维化。
- The authors' lesson was about timing: a precisely timed move from a TNF-rich room to a TGF-beta-rich room. Break the timing, and scar wins.  
  作者的结论关乎时机：从富含 TNF 的环境，按精确的时间转向富含 TGF-β 的环境。打乱时机，瘢痕就赢了。

## 3  The TGF-beta lock · 三  TGF-β 之锁


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Martin 2000 Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys</sub>

- Radiation doctors met TGF-beta long before muscle biologists did.  
  放疗医生认识 TGF-β，比肌肉生物学家早得多。
- In 2000, Martin, Lefaix and Delanian, radiobiologists and clinicians in France, reviewed radiation fibrosis, mostly in skin and the tissue beneath it.  
  2000 年，法国的放射生物学家与临床医生 Martin、Lefaix 和 Delanian 综述了放射性纤维化，主要涉及皮肤及皮下组织。
- They asked why scar-making cells, the myofibroblasts, stay switched on for years.  
  他们追问：为什么制造瘢痕的细胞——肌成纤维细胞——会多年保持开启？
- Their answer was a memorable phrase. Fibrosis may be a wound where the signals for repair never stop.  
  他们的回答是一句令人难忘的话：纤维化，可能是一个修复信号永不停歇的伤口。
- In other words, the scar is not a finished event. It is a process that keeps being told to continue.  
  换句话说，瘢痕不是一件已经结束的事，而是一个不断被命令“继续”的过程。
- And among those signals, they called TGF-beta one the master switch of the fibrotic program.  
  在这些信号中，他们把 TGF-β1 称为纤维化程序的总开关。
- The question mark in their title was honest. It was a strong case, not a closed one.  
  他们标题里的问号是诚实的：这是一桩证据有力的案子，但还没结案。
- They also argued that this pathway might be a specific target for drugs against fibrosis. Hold that thought for our final episode.  
  他们还提出，这条通路可能是抗纤维化药物的一个特异靶点。请把这个想法留到最后一集。

## 4  The supply lines are cut · 四  补给线被切断


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Christov 2007 Mol Biol Cell</sub>

- Every army depends on its supply lines. In muscle, the supply lines are the capillaries.  
  每支军队都依赖补给线。在肌肉里，补给线就是毛细血管。
- In 2007, Christov, Romain Gherardi and their colleagues in France noticed where the guardians choose to live.  
  2007 年，法国的 Christov、Romain Gherardi 和同事注意到了守护者选择住在哪里。
- In mouse and in human muscle, satellite cells sat strikingly close to capillaries.  
  在小鼠和人的肌肉里，卫星细胞都明显紧挨着毛细血管。
- Fibers with more capillaries had more satellite cells, and athletes, whose capillaries increase, fit that pattern.  
  毛细血管越多的纤维，卫星细胞也越多；毛细血管增多的运动员，也符合这一规律。
- So did a rare disease, amyopathic dermatomyositis, in which capillaries are lost while the fibers are spared.  
  一种罕见病——无肌病性皮肌炎——也是如此：毛细血管丢失，而纤维完好。
- In a dish, vessel-lining cells fed stem cell growth with growth factors: IGF-1, HGF, basic FGF, PDGF-BB, and VEGF.  
  在培养皿里，血管内皮细胞用多种生长因子滋养干细胞生长：IGF-1、HGF、碱性 FGF、PDGF-BB 和 VEGF。
- And maturing muscle cells sent signals back, encouraging new vessels. A two-way partnership.  
  而正在成熟的肌肉细胞也回传信号，促进新血管生成。这是一种双向的伙伴关系。
- So if the supply line is cut, the guardian loses not only food, but its home.  
  所以，补给线一旦被切断，守护者失去的不只是口粮，还有它的家。
- Keep that partnership in mind as we ask what radiation does to it.  
  带着这对伙伴关系，我们来问：射线对它做了什么？

<sub>参考：Venkatesulu 2018 JACC Basic Transl Sci; Collao 2023; Zhou 2018</sub>

- Does radiation cut it? Across the body, the answer is often yes.  
  射线会切断它吗？放眼全身，答案往往是肯定的。
- A 2018 review from MD Anderson describes radiation injuring the lining of vessels, and both starting and speeding up hardening of the arteries.  
  MD 安德森癌症中心 2018 年的一篇综述指出，射线会损伤血管内壁，既能引发、也能加速动脉硬化。
- It notes that long-term survivors of the atomic bombs carry more inflammatory signals in their blood, as if radiation leaves the whole body simmering.  
  综述提到，原子弹爆炸的长期幸存者血液中炎症信号更多，仿佛射线让整个身体一直处在文火慢炖之中。
- In muscle, the evidence is thinner. Collao's mice had fewer endothelial cells at day fifty-six.  
  在肌肉里，证据就单薄些了。Collao 的小鼠在第 56 天时内皮细胞更少。
- In the ninety-gray rats, the electron microscope showed swollen endothelial cells, leaky vessels, and scar wrapped around the vessels.  
  在 90 Gy 的大鼠里，电子显微镜下可见内皮细胞肿胀、血管渗漏，以及包绕血管的瘢痕。

<sub>参考：Siemionow 1999 Plast Reconstr Surg</sub>

- But a fair lawyer shows the jury the witness who disagrees.  
  但公正的律师，也会让陪审团看到持不同意见的证人。
- In 1999, rats given a single eight grays showed no significant change in blood flow through a thin muscle, followed for twelve weeks.  
  1999 年，大鼠接受单次 8 Gy 照射后，在 12 周的随访里，一块薄肌肉的血流没有显著变化。
- Dose makes the poison, once again.  
  又一次：剂量决定毒性。

**🔴 假说 HYPOTHESIS** · <sub>参考：Paris 2001 Science</sub>

- Some scientists suspect that vessels fall first, and stem cells only afterward.  
  有些科学家怀疑：血管先倒下，干细胞随后才倒下。
- In 2001, François Paris and Richard Kolesnick showed exactly that in the mouse intestine.  
  2001 年，François Paris 和 Richard Kolesnick 在小鼠肠道里正好证明了这一点。
- When they blocked the death of vessel-lining cells, with basic FGF or by removing a gene called acid sphingomyelinase, the gut's stem cells were spared.  
  当他们用碱性 FGF、或敲除一个叫酸性鞘磷脂酶的基因，阻止血管内皮细胞死亡时，肠道干细胞得以保全。
- But that was the gut, not muscle. In muscle, vessels first remains a hypothesis, with limited evidence, waiting for its own experiment.  
  但那是肠道，不是肌肉。在肌肉里，“血管先倒”仍是一个证据有限的假说，等着属于它自己的实验。

**🔵 实验台笔记 BENCH NOTE** · <sub>参考：Christov 2007; Collao 2023</sub>

- A note from the bench. To count capillaries, stain CD31 on a cross-section.  
  实验台笔记：数毛细血管，就在横切面上染 CD31。
- Report capillaries per fiber, not only per square millimeter. When fibers shrink, more capillaries crowd into each square, and loss can look like gain.  
  要报告每根纤维的毛细血管数，而不只是每平方毫米的数。纤维变细时，每个方格里挤进更多毛细血管，丢失反倒像增加。
- And a stained vessel is not a working one. Pair the count with a perfusion test if you can.  
  而且被染上的血管不等于在工作的血管。条件允许的话，把计数和灌注检测配合起来。

## 5  The power plants falter · 五  发电厂失灵


**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Caiozzo 2010 Radiat Res; Zhou 2018</sub>

- One more suspect remains, and it lives inside every cell: the mitochondrion, the power plant.  
  还剩最后一个嫌疑人，它住在每个细胞里面：线粒体，也就是发电厂。
- Power plants burn fuel with oxygen, and even on a good day they leak sparks, called reactive oxygen species.  
  发电厂用氧气燃烧燃料，即便在好日子里也会漏出火星，这些火星叫活性氧。
- In cultured satellite cells, five grays or more produced a persistent rise in reactive oxygen and nitrogen species.  
  在培养的卫星细胞里，5 Gy 及以上的剂量会让活性氧和活性氮持续升高。
- In the ninety-gray rats, the mitochondria in muscle were visibly swollen.  
  在 90 Gy 的大鼠里，肌肉中的线粒体明显肿胀。

<sub>参考：O'Connor 2022 Skelet Muscle</sub>

- In 2022, O'Connor, Joe Chakkalakal and their colleagues in Rochester gave juvenile male mice three fractions of 8.2 grays, from knee to foot.  
  2022 年，罗切斯特的 O'Connor、Joe Chakkalakal 和同事，给幼年雄性小鼠从膝到足照射 3 次、每次 8.2 Gy。
- Then each mouse lived for one month in a cage with either a locked running wheel or a free-spinning one.  
  随后，每只小鼠在笼中生活一个月，笼里的跑轮要么锁住，要么可以自由转动。
- The fast EDL muscle lost twenty-seven percent of its specific force, meaning force for its size. The slow soleus lost fourteen percent.  
  快肌 EDL 的比肌力——也就是按大小折算的力量——下降了 27%；慢肌比目鱼肌下降了 14%。
- Overall protein oxidation rose by seventy-three percent, and calcium handling inside the fibers was disturbed.  
  总体蛋白质氧化升高了 73%，纤维内的钙调控也被打乱。
- Then came the hopeful part. Mice given a running wheel for a month did better.  
  接着是让人看到希望的部分：给了一个月跑轮的小鼠，状况更好。
- In the EDL, specific force improved by thirty-seven percent, mitochondrial reactive oxygen fell by half, and PGC-1 alpha, a builder of mitochondria, rose.  
  在 EDL 里，比肌力提高了 37%，线粒体活性氧降低一半，而线粒体的建造者 PGC-1α 升高了。
- Those sparks were measured with a glowing dye called MitoSOX. Such dyes are useful, but they can be fooled, so careful labs confirm with a second method.  
  这些火星是用一种叫 MitoSOX 的荧光染料测的。这类染料很有用，但也会被干扰，所以严谨的实验室会用第二种方法确认。

**🟠 关联 ASSOCIATION**

- The authors were careful. These changes correlated with better function. Correlation is a witness, not a confession.  
  作者很谨慎：这些变化与功能改善相关。相关只是证人，不是供词。
- Still, it is the first hint tonight that some of this damage might be pushed back. We will return to it.  
  不过，这是今晚第一条提示：部分损伤也许能被推回去。我们之后还会回到这里。

**🟢 事实 FACT** · <sub>参考：Amorim 2020 Metabolism</sub>

- In 2020, a team in Sydney gave mice six grays and followed their metabolism.  
  2020 年，悉尼的一个团队给小鼠照射 6 Gy，并追踪了它们的代谢。
- Their muscles had fewer oxidative fibers, lower mitochondrial enzyme activity, and burned fat poorly. Glucose uptake was impaired.  
  它们的肌肉里氧化型纤维更少，线粒体酶活性更低，燃烧脂肪的能力变差，葡萄糖摄取也受损。
- Most striking, satellite cells taken from irradiated mice kept the same defects when grown in a dish, far from the beam.  
  最引人注目的是：从受照小鼠取出的卫星细胞，在远离射线的培养皿里生长，依然保留着同样的缺陷。
- The cells remembered.  
  这些细胞记住了。
- And childhood cancer survivors treated with total body irradiation showed a parallel lipid signature in their blood. That is an association.  
  而接受过全身照射的儿童肿瘤幸存者，血液里出现了相似的脂质特征。这属于关联。
- Remember too that Collao's FAPs had turned toward glycolysis. The power problem may not belong to one cell type alone.  
  也别忘了，Collao 的 FAP 已经转向糖酵解。能量问题可能不只属于某一种细胞。

**🔴 假说 HYPOTHESIS** · <sub>参考：Collao 2023; Collao 2025; Lemos 2015; Christov 2007; O'Connor 2022</sub>

- So let us lay the evidence on the table, and build a working model. Mark it red. It is a hypothesis.  
  那就把证据摊在桌上，搭一个工作模型。给它标上红色：这是一个假说。
- The beam strikes. The fiber survives, but satellite cells are lost, and the deepest sleepers thin out.  
  射线打来。纤维挺住了，但卫星细胞流失，沉睡最深的那批变得稀少。
- Senescence spreads through the resident cells, and the rumor mill opens for business.  
  衰老在常驻细胞中蔓延，谣言工厂开张营业。
- Monocytes and macrophages send TGF-beta to the FAPs. Perhaps the TNF that should send surplus FAPs home never comes in time. That part is untested.  
  单核细胞和巨噬细胞向 FAP 发送 TGF-β。也许本该把多余 FAP 送回家的 TNF，始终没有按时到来——这一点尚未检验。
- Senescent, fibrogenic FAPs lay matrix, and their secretions hold back the remaining stem cells.  
  衰老的、促纤维化的 FAP 铺下基质，它们的分泌物又拖住剩下的干细胞。
- Capillaries thin, and the guardians lose their home. Mitochondria leak sparks, and the fibers weaken.  
  毛细血管变稀，守护者失去了家；线粒体漏出火星，纤维变弱。
- Each failure feeds the next. A wound where the signals for repair never stop. That is how weak and stiff could arrive together, years later.  
  每一处失败都喂养着下一处。一个修复信号永不停歇的伤口。这或许就是“变弱”和“变硬”为何会在多年后一同到来。
- No single suspect did it alone. The neighbors, once helpers, turned together.  
  没有哪个嫌疑人单独作案。这些曾经的帮手，是一起变了脸。
- But I promised to tell you where the evidence runs out.  
  但我答应过你，要告诉你证据在哪里断了。
- A good model is honest about its holes, and ours has three large ones.  
  好的模型会坦白自己的漏洞，而我们的模型有三个大漏洞。
- No full time course of macrophages in irradiated muscle. No living test of clearing senescent cells there.  
  受照肌肉里，巨噬细胞的完整时间进程还没有；清除衰老细胞的活体实验也还没有。
- We know the cleanup crew sends TGF-beta. We do not know when it arrives, how long it stays, or whether TNF ever has its turn.  
  我们知道清理队会发送 TGF-β，却不知道它何时到达、停留多久，也不知道 TNF 是否轮到过登场。
- Little direct evidence on muscle vessels. And no one knows which falls first, the vessel or the stem cell.  
  关于肌肉血管的直接证据很少。也没有人知道，先倒下的是血管，还是干细胞。
- The answer matters, because it tells us where a treatment should aim first.  
  这个答案很重要，因为它决定了治疗应该首先瞄准哪里。
- Nearly all of tonight's witnesses were mice and rats, with doses from six to ninety grays.  
  今晚几乎所有的证人都是小鼠和大鼠，剂量从 6 Gy 到 90 Gy 不等。
- Young animals and adult ones, single blows and fractions, days and months. Their stories rhyme, but they are not the same story.  
  幼年与成年，单次与分次，几天与几个月。它们的故事押着韵，却不是同一个故事。
- A patient is not a mouse. And in the human clinic, some clues refuse to fit.  
  病人不是小鼠。而在人类的诊室里，有些线索偏偏对不上。
- In some rats, the stem cells seem to wake rather than vanish. And the tumor itself may be sending messages to the muscle.  
  在某些大鼠身上，干细胞似乎是被唤醒，而不是消失；而肿瘤本身，也许正在给肌肉寄信。
- In our final episode, we leave the bench for the bedside.  
  最后一集，我们离开实验台，走向病床。
- The human evidence, the models we trust, the clues that contradict, the attempts to break the lock, and the cases still open.  
  人体证据、我们所依赖的模型、相互矛盾的线索、开锁的尝试，以及仍未结的悬案。
- Until then, my friends, judge the neighbors by their deeds, and the evidence by its weight.  
  在那之前，朋友们，看邻居要看所作所为，看证据要看分量。
- The references for tonight's episode are on the screen.  
  本集的参考文献，都在屏幕上。
